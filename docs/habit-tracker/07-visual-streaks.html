HT-301 Freeze moment — how it's communicated

❄️
Streak protected
You missed yesterday, so a streak freeze kept your 17 days safe. 1 freeze left this month.
🛟
Out of freezes
Next miss resets this streak. Tip: shrink the habit rather than lose it — even 5 minutes counts.
  • Freezes apply automatically — no purchase, no chore. 2 per month per habit, reset monthly.
  • Always visible, never silent: the dashboard, heatmap and hero all show frozen days distinctly. The user is protected, not deceived.
  • When freezes run out, the message pivots to the reduce-load suggestion (Phase 2's AI coach picks this up) — shrinking a habit is framed as smart, not as failure.
  • Not available on medication items (HT-801 — adherence is a record, not a game).

HT-104 Engine rules — single source of truth

  • All streak math lives in backend queries.py — mobile and web display, never recompute. (Same discipline as the 90-day expiry threshold constant.)
  • The check-in log is truth; StreakState is a cache recomputed on every write.
  • Days are the user's local dates (IANA timezone on profile). Travel rule, fixed early and never revisited: the local date at the moment of check-in wins; history is never retro-edited by timezone changes.
  • Weekly-quota habits (read 4×/week): streak = consecutive weeks hitting quota, displayed in weeks.
  • Interval habits (water 5×/day): the day counts as done at a configurable fraction (default 80% of windows).
💡 Test checklist for HT-104: month boundary freeze reset · freeze + repair same week · quota week spanning a vacation · DST shift · timezone flight crossing midnight · archived habit mid-streak. All in the Sprint 1 pytest suite.
