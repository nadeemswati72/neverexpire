The Problem

Documents Expire.
Lives Are Disrupted.

In the UAE, expired documents don't just cause inconvenience — they cause legal status loss, financial penalties, and business shutdown. Yet no purpose-built solution exists for this market.

🛂

Visa & Residency Expiry

UAE residence visas must be renewed every 2–3 years. Overstay by one day and penalties begin immediately. Many expats discover the expiry only when denied at immigration or when applying for a new job.

AED 200/day overstay fine
🪪

Emirates ID Lapse

Emirates ID is mandatory for every UAE resident. An expired EID blocks access to hospitals, banks, government services, and property contracts. Renewals take 3–5 working days — timing is critical.

AED 200 fine after 30 days
🚗

Vehicle Registration (Mulkiya)

Driving with an expired Mulkiya in UAE is a criminal offence. Many residents are unaware their vehicle registration and insurance can expire in different months. A traffic stop means instant fine and impound.

AED 400 fine + vehicle impound
🏢

Business Document Chaos

SMEs with 5–50 employees track visa renewals, trade licenses, equipment certifications, and professional licenses across spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups. One expired employee work permit can halt operations and trigger a Ministry of Labour ban.

AED 50,000 fine + business ban

The Product

Built. Working.
Ready to Scale.

NeverExpire V2 is not a concept. It is a live, working product — web app, iOS app, and AI extraction pipeline — all built and running today.

🤖

AI Document Extraction

Take a photo of any document — passport, Emirates ID, driving license. Our Claude AI reads the expiry date, document number, and holder name automatically. No typing. 90%+ accuracy confirmed in testing.

📊

Smart Dashboard

Colour-coded status at a glance: red = expired, amber = expiring soon, green = valid. Sorted by urgency. One view for your entire family or entire company. Works on web and iPhone today.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Multi-Person Management

Add your spouse, children, domestic staff, or employees as separate profiles. Each person's documents tracked independently. Switch between family members in one tap.

🔔

WhatsApp Reminders (Sprint 7)

90 days, 60 days, 30 days, 7 days, 1 day before expiry — WhatsApp message sent automatically. UAE has 95% WhatsApp penetration. No new app to install for the reminder itself.

🔒

Secure Document Storage

Encrypted file storage with automatic watermarking for shared copies. JWT-authenticated API. Every document copy is watermarked with the owner's name before sharing — prevents misuse.
