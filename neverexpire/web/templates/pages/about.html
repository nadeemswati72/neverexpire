NeverExpire

Never miss a renewal, expiry, or deadline again — for you and your family.

What is NeverExpire?

NeverExpire is a personal and family document tracker. Upload a photo or PDF of any document — a passport, insurance policy, warranty, medication, certificate, or subscription — and AI automatically reads the key details so you don't have to type them in. NeverExpire keeps track of issue and expiry dates for every member of your family and reminds you before anything lapses.

How It Works

  1. Add a document — upload a photo/PDF, or enter the details manually.
  2. AI extracts the details — title, dates, document number, issuing authority, and other key fields are read automatically.
  3. Review & confirm — check the extracted information and edit anything before saving.
  4. Tracked per family member — every document is linked to a person in your household.
  5. Stay ahead of deadlines — the dashboard highlights what's expiring soon or already expired.

Current Features

  • AI-powered document extraction using Claude
  • Family member profiles with per-person document tracking
  • Dashboard with "Expiring Soon" and "Expired" views
  • Manual entry mode for documents without a scan
  • Admin-configurable document types and custom fields
  • Document watermarking for safer sharing
  • Role-based access for admins and users

Benefits

  • Peace of mind — never get caught off guard by an expired document again
  • One place for your whole family's important documents
  • Minimal manual data entry, thanks to AI extraction
  • You stay in control of your data
  • Built to grow — ready for future compliance and platform needs
