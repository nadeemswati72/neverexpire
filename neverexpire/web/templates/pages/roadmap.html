Road Map

Where NeverExpire is headed, phase by phase.

Phase 1 — Foundation

Done

The core experience, live today.

  • Core document tracking with expiry dates
  • AI-powered extraction with Claude
  • Family member management
  • Admin-configurable document types
  • Dashboard with expiry reminders

Phase 2 — Smarter Input & Trust

Done

Richer extraction and more ways to add documents.

  • Expanded AI extraction: document number, issuing authority, holder name, and more
  • Manual entry mode — add documents without uploading a file
  • Document watermarking for safer sharing
  • About and Road Map pages

Phase 3 — Reach & Accessibility

In Progress

Taking NeverExpire beyond the browser.

  • Voice integration for adding and querying documents
  • Android & iOS companion apps
  • Push notifications for upcoming expiries

Phase 4 — Advanced Reminders & Document Intelligence

Planned

Reminders and document support that adapt to you.

  • Recursive/recurring reminders for documents that renew on a schedule
  • Enhanced document type support and broader extraction coverage
  • Smart suggestions based on your document history

Phase 5 — Compliance & Trust

Planned

Meeting data protection standards.

  • GDPR / PDPL compliance tooling (data export & deletion, consent tracking)
  • Audit logging
  • Stronger security: encryption at rest, two-factor authentication
  • PCI DSS compliance for handling payment data

Phase 6 — Public Deployment & Monetization

Planned

Taking NeverExpire live for everyone.

  • Production hosting & deployment (beyond the local dev server and SQLite)
  • Subscription plans & premium tiers
  • Payment integration and billing
  • Public sign-up, onboarding, and usage analytics
