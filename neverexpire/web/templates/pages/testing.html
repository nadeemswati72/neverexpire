NeverExpire Testing Guide

Everything you need to test the NeverExpire website and the NeverExpire Mobile app for this round.

Temporary page — this guide is for the current testing round and will be removed afterwards.

Before You Start

  • The website and the mobile app are two separate apps with separate demo accounts — that's expected for this round, not a bug.
  • Every demo account on both apps uses the same password: Demo@1234
  • To use the mobile app, or the AI document scan inside it, your phone needs to be on the same Wi-Fi network as the developer's computer.
  • On a different network and can't reach the mobile app? No problem — skip ahead to Mobile App Screenshots, look through each screen, and send your feedback based on those instead.

2. Testing the Mobile App

On a different network than the developer? You won't be able to open the app on your phone. Scroll down to Mobile App Screenshots instead, review each screen, and let us know what you think.

Set Up

  1. Install Expo Go from the Play Store (Android) or App Store (iOS).
  2. Make sure your phone is on the same Wi-Fi network as the developer's computer.
  3. Ask the developer for the QR code or the exp://<ip>:8081 link shown in their terminal, and open it with Expo Go.
  4. The app bundle compiles on first connect — this can take a minute.

What to Test

  • Dashboard — greeting, summary cards (tap through to a filtered document list), recent documents
  • Add Document (the centre "+" button)
    • Take Photo, Choose from Gallery, or Upload File — the app sends the image to the same AI extraction the website uses and pre-fills the form (document type, name, dates, document number, issuer, notes). Review the pre-filled fields, fix anything that's wrong, then save.
    • Enter details manually skips the AI step entirely
  • Document Details — view, edit, and delete (with confirmation) a document
  • My Documents — search box and status filter chips
  • Family — member list with document counts, add a new member, open a member's documents
  • Profile — edit account details, "Change Password" (UI-only), log out
  • Notifications (bell icon) — documents that are expiring soon or expired
  • Side drawer (hamburger icon) — Settings, Help & Support, About, Logout

Known Limitations

  • The mobile app's family members and documents are a separate, on-device demo dataset — they are not shared with the website.
  • The AI document scan needs the phone and the developer's computer on the same Wi-Fi network. If it can't reach the server you'll see a message and can still fill in the form manually.
  • Camera capture needs a real device — most emulators don't have a working camera.
  • "Change Password" and the notification toggles in Settings are UI-only and don't persist or send anything.
  • Reinstalling the app or clearing its storage resets documents and family members back to the seed data.

Troubleshooting

_ExpoFontLoader.default.getLoadedFonts is not a function on first load — already fixed in this build. If you re-clone the project and see it again, run npx expo install --fix and then npx expo start -c.

"Project is incompatible with this version of Expo Go" — the Expo Go app on your phone doesn't support the SDK version this project uses. Update Expo Go from the store, or ask the developer for guidance.

3. Mobile App Screenshots

Every screen of the mobile app, in the order you'd normally move through them. If you couldn't open the app yourself, look through these and tell us what you think of the layout, wording, and overall feel — that feedback is just as valuable as testing on a device.

1. Login

The first screen you see. Enter your email and password to sign in, or tap a name in the "Demo accounts" card to auto-fill the email — the password is the same for everyone (Demo@1234).

2. Dashboard

Your home screen after logging in. Greets you by name and shows two quick-glance cards (Expiring Soon, All Documents) plus a Recent Documents list, color-coded red/orange/green for expired, expiring soon, and valid. Tap "See All" for the full list.

3. Side Drawer (Menu)

Opened with the hamburger icon in the top-left of Dashboard or Family. Shows your profile at the top and links to every other screen: Add Document, My Documents, Family Members, Notifications, Settings, Help & Support, About, Testing Guide, and Logout.

4. Profile

Opened by tapping your name and avatar at the top of the side drawer. Shows your account details (editable via "Edit") and a Change Password form. The password change is UI-only for this round and doesn't actually update anything.

5. Family Members

Reached via the Family tab at the bottom. Lists everyone in the family with an avatar, name, relationship (Wife, Son, Daughter, etc.), and how many documents they have. Tap a card to see that person's documents, or the "+" icon to add a new family member.

6. Family Member's Documents

Opened by tapping a person on Family Members. Shows every document belonging to that person — type, document number, and expiry status — in one place.

7. Notifications

Opened by tapping the bell icon on the Dashboard. Lists every document across the family that has expired or is expiring soon, so you can see at a glance what needs attention.

8. Add Document — Choose Source

Opened with the green "+" button in the middle of the bottom tab bar. Choose Take Photo, Choose from Gallery, or Upload File to scan a document — the app sends the image to the same AI extraction the website uses and pre-fills the form. Or tap "Enter details manually instead" to skip the scan.

9. Document Form

Where you review (after a scan) or enter (manually) a document's details: Document Type, who it "Belongs To", then Full Name, Document Number, Issued Date, Expiry Date, Issued By, and Notes. After an AI scan, these fields arrive pre-filled for you to check and correct.

10. My Documents

Reached from the side drawer or the Dashboard's "See All" link. Shows every document for the whole family, with a search box and filter chips (All / Expiring Soon / Expired / Valid) to narrow the list down.

11. Document Details

Opened by tapping any document row. Shows the document's photo (or a placeholder if none was attached), its type and status badge, all of its details, any notes, and buttons to Edit or Delete the document — deleting asks for confirmation first.

Reporting Issues

For each issue, please note: which app (website or mobile), the screen and action, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. Screenshots help a lot.
